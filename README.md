## How to use the evidence

1. Filter a CSV by institution, campus or unit, and activity. Read the authority field before comparing positions. A teaching-center example, an approved pilot and an examination regulation carry different weight.
2. Read permission and endorsement separately. The conditions, rationale and evaluation fields explain what an institution required, what it hoped to achieve and what researchers tested. General access to a tool establishes only that level of access.
3. Open the original source at the listed section. Use the archived version when available. Multiple sources can share a finding ID. Cambridge and Nottingham, for example, appear in the same OpRaise study. Reports of that study share an evidence base.

## Selection and limitations

Both databases cover a baseline of 54 university or campus entries. Fifty were named as policy actors in the exam-device guide. Donghua University and China University of Petroleum (East China) hosted CET notices, Duke appeared through a research publication, and Carnegie Mellon hosted the MIT reading. The faculty dataset also distinguishes the predecessor University of Adelaide from Adelaide University and includes three further study sites. Faculty assessment starting points included the MIT committee report, Harvard Bok Center, NYU SPS, Melbourne, UCL and LSE.
