This checkpoint does not establish coverage or prevalence across the cohort. For the six QS top-100 institutions with new or corroborated findings in this checkpoint, Purdue, UCD, PolyU and NUS explicitly assign responsibility for marking, feedback or both to staff, with different operational rules. Purdue asks for maximum feasible human involvement and defendable grades. UCD names an approved examiner and requires advance disclosure. NUS generally requires human review with an explicit exception for objective or closed-ended marking and expects spot checks. PolyU keeps teachers responsible for grades and feedback and separately restricts data uploads. Seoul National’s nonbinding advice discourages complete reliance, and UNSW’s guidance generally bars AI marking while its teaching advice describes conditional feedback analysis. The [evidence table](faculty-ai-evidence.csv) supplies finding IDs and passages for these six institutions. Outside the cutoff, Helsinki and Cyber University are logged separately, with the grounds for inclusion in the coverage table.
